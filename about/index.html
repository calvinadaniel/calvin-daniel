About

I'm Calvin Daniel, a web developer and automation builder with more than 12 years of experience helping small businesses turn their websites into working parts of the business. I hand-code fast, mobile-first sites, and I add the booking, intake and reporting automation that keeps inquiries from slipping through.

By day I automate weekly, monthly and quarterly reporting for a global publisher using Power BI, SQL and Power Automate, so I know exactly what manual reporting costs a team. I also build with AI tools like Claude Code and hold Anthropic certifications, which lets me deliver custom work quickly without relying on templates.

Away from the keyboard I run marathons. Three years in, I've finished nine races, including three New York City Marathons, and the same habit shows up in my work: agree on the goal, show up every week, and finish.
