# Pricing — Calvin Daniel

Last updated: 2026-10-20

Starting prices for custom websites and light automation for local service businesses. Final quotes are confirmed after a free 20-minute site review.

## Site That Books

- Price: from $1,750 (one-time)
- Timeline: typically 3 to 4 weeks
- Best for: Solo practitioners and local service businesses
- Includes:
  - Custom mobile-first website (no templates)
  - Service pages
  - Booking or inquiry form
  - Basic SEO
  - Hosting handoff

## Site + Automation

- Price: from $3,500 (one-time)
- Timeline: typically 4 to 6 weeks
- Best for: Businesses that already get inquiries but lose some of them
- Includes:
  - Everything in Site That Books
  - Inquiry routing to the right person
  - Automatic replies
  - Weekly summary of leads

## Reporting Automation

- Price: from $1,500 (scoped after audit)
- Timeline: depends on data sources and tools
- Best for: Teams that rebuild the same weekly or monthly report by hand
- Includes:
  - Process audit
  - Dashboards and scheduled refresh on tools you already use (for example Power BI, SQL, Power Automate)

## Free site review

- Price: $0
- Duration: 20 minutes
- What you get: A map of where inquiries drop off and a clear recommendation for what to build
